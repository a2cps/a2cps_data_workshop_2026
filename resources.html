Data access

NIMH Data Archive

A2CPS data are maintained in the National Institute of Mental Health Data Archive (NDA). Any researcher can access them by completing a Data Access Request for the NDA permission group. In NDA, A2CPS is listed as “Data Center for Acute to Chronic Pain Biosignatures”, collection #5121.

A2CPS NDA study page (ID 3307) ↗ Access guide on a2cps.org ↗

Start early. Access requests must be reviewed and approved. Filing yours before the workshop means you can follow the hands-on segments with real data.

  1. 1Log in or create an NDA account
    Sign in with any of the three NIH Researcher Auth Service (RAS) options, or create an account in NDA.    nda.nih.gov ↗
  2. 2Open the Data Permissions Dashboard
    You must be signed in. Find the “NDA” permission group in the list.    Dashboard ↗
  3. 3Request access
    In the Actions column for the NDA permission group, click “Request Access” to start the Data Access Request.    Details ↗
  4. 4Explore and download
    Browse the A2CPS study page, add data to a package, and download with NDA tools. Webinars and tutorials on nda.nih.gov walk through each step.    Study 3307 ↗

Papers & citation

Reference papers and how to cite A2CPS

Two papers describe the program and should be cited when referring to the A2CPS protocol and biomarkers.

Biomarker paperSluka KA, Wager TD, Sutherland SP, Labosky PA, Balach T, et al. Predicting chronic postsurgical pain: current evidence and a novel program to develop predictive biomarker signatures. PAIN, 2023.
Protocol paperBerardi G, Frey-Law L, Sluka KA, Bayman EO, Coffey CS, et al. Multi-Site Observational Study to Assess Biomarkers for Susceptibility or Resilience to Chronic Pain: The Acute to Chronic Pain Signatures (A2CPS) Study Protocol. Frontiers in Medicine, 2022.

Attribution statement

Include the following in publications or presentations that use A2CPS data:

Data were provided [in part] by the A2CPS Consortium funded by the National Institutes of Health (NIH) Common Fund, which is managed by the Office of the Director (OD) / Office of Strategic Coordination (OSC). Consortium components and their associated funding sources include Clinical Coordinating Center (U24NS112873), Data Integration and Resource Center (U54DA049110), Omics Data Generation Centers (U54DA049116, U54DA049115, U54DA049113), Multi-site Clinical Center 1 (MCC1) (UM1NS112874), and Multi-site Clinical Center 2 (MCC2) (UM1NS118922).
