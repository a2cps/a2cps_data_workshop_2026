Introduction to A2CPS

Kathleen Sluka & Tor Wager

How the consortium is organized, what the program set out to do, and the study protocol and timeline: two surgical cohorts followed from before surgery to 12 months after, with biomarkers spanning psychosocial, sensory, omics and brain-imaging domains.

  • Organization: clinical centers, omics centers, the data integration and resource center, and the clinical coordinating center
  • Goals: biomarkers and signatures that predict susceptibility or resilience to chronic pain after surgery
  • Protocol and timeline

Data highlights

Briha Ansari, Jamie, Patrick Sadil, Margaret Taub and others

Greatest hits of the baseline data: short presentations of findings that show what the dataset can do.

  • Brain-wide association studies (BWAS) across image-derived phenotypes
  • Brain age and its psychosocial, sensory and omics correlates
  • Comparison of cohorts and effect sizes across surgical populations
  • Immune transcriptome signatures, multi-omics correlates of fatigue, and more
